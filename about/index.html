About Metricus

The data layer between
AI and business.

Metricus measures how AI represents businesses and makes that data actionable for everyone. The free AI visibility optimizer takes a page URL, shows what the major AI platforms answer when that page is the source, and proposes rewrites that change the answer. Free, no signup, no card. How it works: what you put in, what you get back.

The team

Recommender systems
and AI researchers.

Metricus was founded by researchers whose work spans recommender systems, information retrieval, natural language processing, and applied machine learning. Before building Metricus, the team spent over a decade studying the mechanisms that determine what algorithmic systems surface, and what they bury.

When large language models became the new interface layer between consumers and businesses, we recognized the problem immediately: the same dynamics that govern search ranking and recommendation feeds now govern AI-generated answers.

Team expertise

What the team has worked on.

Recommender Systems

Collaborative filtering, content-based ranking, hybrid recommendation architectures, and the evaluation methods used to measure their accuracy.

Information Retrieval

Retrieval-augmented generation, source selection and ranking in generative systems, and how grounding data propagates into AI-generated answers.

Natural Language Processing

Entity recognition, semantic similarity, and sentiment and stance classification in generated text.

Statistical Methods

Non-parametric hypothesis testing, bootstrap confidence intervals, and effect size estimation for stochastic text outputs.

The way the optimizer reads a page and measures an answer comes out of this research background.
