How it works

You give the Metricus optimizer one thing: the address of a page. It gives back four: the answer an AI assistant gives about that page, the passages of the page behind that answer, a rewrite for each one, and the changes as a file.

What you put in

The address of one page. A product page, a pricing page, a comparison, a guide. Nothing else: no account, no access to your site, no card. The tool opens the page the way any reader opens it.

What you get back

The answer, and the parts of it that are yours

One prompt a buyer would type. The assistant searches and answers with your page among the results it opens. The line above the answer says whether it cited you, named you and said what you wanted said. Sentences that came from your page are marked.

Every passage of your page is marked

Your page, in the order the assistant reads it, with a mark on each passage: the assistant drew on this one and said what you want, it drew on this one and did not. No score to interpret.

A rewrite is waiting on each one

Take the rewrite, type your own over it, put the passage back or drop it. Each change is put to the assistant again, so you see what your wording did before you touch your live page.

Leave with the changes in a file

The page before and after as a diff, a CSV of the changes, the findings as markdown, or print. Hand it to whoever edits the page.

What it does not do

  • It never edits your site. The changes live in your browser until you copy them into your page.
  • It sends nothing to anyone. Nothing is posted or published.
  • If no rewrite is better than what you have, it says so and leaves the passage alone.
  • It does not disclose the method beyond this. What you can verify is the answer, the passages and the rewrites, on your own page.

Questions

How long does it take?

The answer is on screen in well under a minute. The rewrites take a few minutes more, and you read the answer while they are worked out.

Can I stop it?

Yes. The button that started the run stops it, and what you already have stays on screen.

Does it change my live page?

No. The changes live in your browser. You copy what you want into your own page when you are ready.

Which page should I check first?

The page a buyer lands on when they ask the question you care about: a product page, a pricing page, a comparison, a guide. Home pages are usually the weakest source.

What if no rewrite is better than what I have?

It says so and leaves the passage alone. If the page does not support what you asked for at all, it says that instead and asks you to put it on the page first. Either one is an answer.
