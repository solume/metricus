What is generative engine optimization (GEO)?

GEO is the practice of optimizing your brand's content so it gets cited in AI-generated responses from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews. Unlike SEO, which targets search engine rankings, GEO focuses on structured data, entity authority, and citation signals that language models use when selecting brands to recommend.

How do AI chatbots decide which brands to recommend?

AI chatbots select brands based on entity authority, third-party credibility (reviews, awards, expert mentions), and presence on authoritative list pages. Research shows that traditional SEO signals like backlinks have near-zero influence on AI recommendations.

Start by auditing what each AI platform actually says about your brand. Factual errors about brands are common. Fixing errors requires updating the authoritative third-party pages that AI models rely on during retrieval.

What is an AI visibility score?

An AI visibility score measures how often and how favorably your brand appears in AI-generated responses across platforms like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. It typically combines mention frequency, sentiment, accuracy, and competitive share of voice.
