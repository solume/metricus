What is the difference between being mentioned and being recommended by AI?

Mentioned means your name is in the answer. Recommended means AI suggests you as one of the options to try or pick. An answer can mention you in passing, or to say someone else is a better fit, without recommending you. The AI Visibility Score shows both.

How do I read my AI Visibility Score?

Compare the grey number with the blue one. Grey is today, with your website as it is. Blue is with your rewritten website.

Big moves matter more than small ones, since AI answers vary from one ask to the next. The recommend numbers are closest to sales, since that is when AI tells people to try you. The mention numbers show whether AI brings you up at all.

If the numbers go down, try another edit of the answer or another part of your website. If they stay flat, edit the answer differently.

For a steadier picture, run a second question in a new session. When both questions move the same way, you can trust the direction.

What is AI visibility?

AI visibility is how often, and how well, AI assistants mention and recommend your business when people ask for what you offer. You can count two parts of it: whether AI names you, and whether it recommends you as an option to try. You read a third part: how AI describes you when it does.

It matters because people ask AI what to buy, and AI answers from websites, like yours. If your website is unclear, AI may leave you out.

What is GEO, and is it just SEO with a new name?

GEO, or generative engine optimization, is the work of getting your business named in AI answers. You also see it called AEO (answer engine optimization), AI SEO or LLM SEO. It overlaps with SEO a lot: a clear website, useful content and other websites that mention you help with both.

The difference is the result you aim for. SEO aims at a place in a list of links. GEO aims at a sentence in an answer.

That also changes how you check your work. A rank tracker tells you where your website sits in a list. For AI you have to look at the answer itself.
