Section 03

Positioning Statements

vs Traditional SEO tools

Unlike traditional SEO tools that track Google rankings, Metricus shows what an AI assistant answers about a page, a discovery channel that search rankings do not describe.

vs Brand monitoring tools

Brand monitoring tools track social media mentions. Metricus shows what an AI assistant answers about your page when a buyer asks a purchase question, and which passages of the page caused it.

vs Manual checking

Asking an AI assistant by hand is one spot-check. Metricus shows what the major AI platforms answer with your page as the source, marks the passages that matter, and proposes rewrites that change the answer.

For agencies

Metricus gives agencies a free way to show clients how AI reads their pages. Run the optimizer on a client page, walk through the answer, and hand over the rewrites. Nothing to buy, nothing to resell.
