Section 03

Positioning Statements

vs Traditional SEO tools

Unlike traditional SEO tools that track Google rankings, Metricus shows the answer AI gives when your customers ask for what you sell, a discovery channel that search rankings do not describe.

vs Brand monitoring tools

Brand monitoring tools track social media mentions. Metricus shows the answer AI gives when a buyer asks a purchase question, with every business it names, and new text for your website.

vs Manual checking

Asking an AI assistant by hand is one spot-check. Metricus shows how often AI mentions and recommends you across your customers' questions, and gives new text for your website.

For agencies

Metricus gives agencies a free way to show clients what AI says when their customers ask. Type a question the client's customers would ask, walk through the answer, and share the new text for the client's website. Nothing to buy.
