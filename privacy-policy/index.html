Privacy policy

Who we are

Metricus ("we", "us") operates metricusapp.com. This policy says what we collect and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • The questions you type, the website address you pick or add, your edits to the answer, and the answers, new text and scores Metricus shows you. We keep these to improve Metricus.
  • Your email address, only if you leave it to get your scores after 7 days, write to us or use the contact form.
  • Usage data from analytics, described below.

How we use it

  • To run Metricus and show you the result.
  • To check your website again after 7 days and email you the scores, if you left your email.
  • To improve Metricus.
  • To answer you when you write to us.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties for marketing.

Third-party services

  • Google Analytics and Google Ads, for website analytics (policies.google.com/privacy)
  • Microsoft Clarity, for website analytics (privacy.microsoft.com)
  • GitHub Pages, for website hosting
  • Cloudflare, for the service that runs Metricus

Cookies

The analytics services above use cookies to understand how visitors use the site: pages viewed, traffic sources, general engagement. You can block them in your browser.

Retention

We keep the questions, addresses and results for as long as they are useful for improving Metricus. You can ask for deletion at any time by emailing us.

Your rights

You may ask for access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time at metricusapp@gmail.com.

Changes

Changes to this policy are posted on this page with the updated date.
