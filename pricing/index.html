There is no price.

Metricus is one free tool. Put in a URL. You see the answer an AI assistant gives about your page, the passages of the page that caused it, and a rewrite for each one. Nothing on this site is sold.

What every run gives you

  • The answer an AI assistant gives with your page among its sources, and the line that says whether it cited you, named you and said what you wanted said.
  • Every passage of your page marked: toward what you want, or away from it.
  • A rewrite for each marked passage. Take it, edit it, put the passage back or drop it. Each change is put to the assistant again.
  • The changes as a file: the page before and after, a CSV of the changes, the findings as markdown, or print.

Why there is no price

Metricus wants the tool run on real pages. What we learn from those runs is worth more to us right now than a sale would be.

There is no paid tier behind it, no trial that expires, and no limit on how many pages you run.

Questions

Yes. Every page, no fee per client. Run it on their page and show them the answer before and after.

No. Everything the tool does, it does for free.
