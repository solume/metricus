12 AI Visibility Tools Compared: Which One Is Worth Your Money in 2026

Metricus Research · March 14, 2026 · 8 min read

The 2026 tool landscape

The AI visibility tools market in 2026 spans three distinct categories, from enterprise SEO platforms adding AI features to purpose-built startups. The right tool depends on your workflow, budget, and how often you need to check. This comparison covers 12 tools across all three categories.

3 categories of tools

Purpose-built AI visibility platforms (Otterly AI, Scrunch AI, Peec AI) focus exclusively on tracking AI mentions. Enterprise SEO suites (BrightEdge, Conductor, Semrush Copilot) are adding AI visibility as a feature within broader platforms. DIY approaches (manual ChatGPT testing, custom scripts) offer zero cost but limited reliability.

Deciding factors

What we found matters most when choosing: measurement method (API calls vs. real browser sessions produce different results), platform coverage (some tools only cover 2–3 AI platforms while buyers use many), pricing model (monthly subscriptions vs. annual enterprise contracts), and output format (dashboards for ongoing monitoring vs. a one-off check for a baseline). The right tool for a startup running its first visibility check is different from what an enterprise SEO team adding AI tracking to an existing workflow needs.

What separates good tools from bad ones

What we found when evaluating AI visibility tools is that the measurement method matters more than the feature set. Tools that query AI through developer APIs return different results than what real users see. API responses often lack web-search grounding, miss platform-specific features like AI Overviews, and cannot capture the actual user experience. Tools that simulate real browser sessions — opening a browser, navigating to ChatGPT, typing a query, reading the response — produce results that reflect what buyers actually encounter.

The second critical differentiator is prompt coverage. Some tools test 10–15 generic prompts. Others test 50–100+ prompts designed to reflect actual buyer behavior in your specific category. What we found is that narrow prompt sets miss the most valuable insights: the specific query types where your brand is invisible. A brand might appear in broad “best tool” queries but be completely absent from industry-specific or use-case-specific questions — and a narrow prompt set would never reveal that gap.

Third, platform coverage varies dramatically. Some tools only track ChatGPT. Others cover ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini. The most comprehensive tools cover eight or more platforms including Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, Copilot, and Google AI Overviews. Since different buyers use different AI platforms, limited coverage means limited visibility into your actual competitive position.

By use case

First baseline: Checking a handful of buyer questions by hand costs nothing but gives one sample per question. A page-level tool shows the answer an AI assistant gives for one page and which passages on the page caused it, and it is free. Active optimization campaigns: Monthly monitoring tools offer the feedback loop needed for iterative improvement. Agency client work: Monitoring subscriptions accumulate across a client portfolio, so most agencies add them only for clients running active campaigns. Enterprise SEO teams: Platform add-ons from existing SEO suites minimize tool sprawl.

Common Questions About AI Visibility Tools

When is a one-time check enough, and when do I need a subscription?

A one-time check is enough when you need a baseline of how the major AI platforms describe your brand before deciding whether monitoring is worth paying for, or when an agency is evaluating a new client. A subscription earns its cost once you are changing pages and need weekly feedback on the effect.

Which tools offer coverage across multiple channels and AI platforms?

Most subscription tools cover 3-5 platforms in their base plans and charge extra for additional platform access. Enterprise suites such as BrightEdge focus on Google AI Overviews. Check the platform list against the assistants your buyers use before paying.

How do I check the accuracy and correctness of brand information in AI?

Ask the major AI platforms the questions your buyers ask and compare the answers against your own pricing, features, positioning and competitive claims. Monitoring tools log those answers over time, so a wrong fact that keeps coming back is easier to spot than in a single manual check.

What tools measure competitive positioning in AI recommendations?

The subscription trackers (Otterly AI, Scrunch AI, Peec AI) record which brands the major AI platforms name for a set of prompts and how that share moves over time. Enterprise SEO suites are adding the same kind of tracking as a feature inside a broader platform.

What credibility and trust indicators do LLMs use when recommending brands?

LLMs weigh several trust signals when deciding which brands to recommend: multi-platform web presence, pricing transparency, third-party reviews and citations, structured data, and consistent factual information across sources.
