Profound vs Peec AI (2026): Which AI Visibility Platform Fits You?

Metricus Research · August 5, 2026 · 4 min read

Choose Profound if you are an enterprise that needs first-party AI-crawler data, a 1.3B+ real-query dataset, ten-engine coverage and SOC 2 compliance, and you have a funded, dedicated AEO program. Choose Peec AI if you want clean self-serve prompt tracking with unlimited seats, starting at $95/mo, and your buyers live in ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google's AI surfaces. The headline looks like a $99 vs $95 tie, but they are different tools: Profound's real multi-engine entry is ~$399/mo (Growth); Peec's is $95/mo with a three-engine cap. Neither rewrites your pages — for that, pair either with a free AI visibility optimizer. Pricing verified against vendor pages the week of August 3, 2026.

Data depth and first-party crawler intelligence — edge: Profound

Profound's Agent Analytics reads real AI-crawler behavior on your own site, and its citation intelligence is powered by a 1.3B+ real-query dataset rather than clickstream proxies. For a large brand that needs to know not just whether it is cited but which crawler fetched what and when, that first-party layer is the widest gap between the two products, and Peec has no equivalent.

Pricing and seat economics — edge: Peec

Peec includes unlimited seats on every tier — $95, $245 or $495 — which means an agency or a cross-functional team pays the same as a single user. Profound prices by tier with limited seats, its entry tier covers ChatGPT only, and its realistic multi-engine entry is ~$399/mo. For any team below enterprise scale, Peec's economics win outright.

Engine coverage and add-on math — edge: split

Profound reaches up to ten engines, but only on custom Enterprise; its self-serve tiers cover one to three. Peec offers seven engines but caps every self-serve tier at three of them, with a fourth costing $35–$165/mo and Claude and GPT-5 Search locked to Enterprise. Neither gives broad coverage cheaply; Profound's ceiling is higher, Peec's floor is more honest.

Actionability — edge: Profound

Profound's Agents (Growth and above) generate briefs and drafts from cited gaps, moving it a step past pure measurement. Peec routes findings to external LLMs via MCP but does not act on them itself. Note that neither platform rewrites and re-tests your live page copy — that remains a separate, page-level job.

Enterprise readiness and compliance — edge: Profound

SOC 2, SSO, dedicated support and board-level reporting are native to Profound's enterprise motion. Peec offers comparable controls only on its custom Enterprise tier.

Which should you pick?

Pick Peec AI if you are an agency or lean in-house team that wants clean daily tracking across the three engines that matter to you, with unlimited seats, for under $100/mo. Pick Profound if you run a funded enterprise AEO program that needs crawler-level data, ten-engine reach and compliance. Whichever you choose, both are trackers: to actually rewrite a losing page, pair either with a free page-level optimizer, and if you are weighing the wider field, start with Profound alternatives 2026 and Peec AI alternatives 2026.

FAQ

Profound vs Peec AI — what do they cost in 2026?

Peec: $95/$245/$495 self-serve with unlimited seats. Profound: demo-led pricing; cited figures are ~$99/mo (ChatGPT only), ~$399/mo Growth (three engines), a demo-gated $499/mo Lite, and custom Enterprise for ten engines.

Which is cheaper to start?

Peec at $95/mo for three engines. Profound's ~$99 entry covers ChatGPT only, and its real multi-engine entry is ~$399/mo.

Which has better data?

Profound — first-party Agent Analytics crawler data and a 1.3B+ real-query dataset, versus Peec's clickstream-proxy approach.

Which is better for agencies?

Peec, because unlimited seats on every tier mean whole teams and clients can share one plan.

Which is better for enterprise?

Profound: SOC 2, SSO, ten-engine Enterprise coverage and board-level reporting.

Do either of them rewrite my content?

No. Profound's Agents can draft briefs from gaps (Growth+), but neither rewrites and re-tests your live page copy. Use a free page-level optimizer for that job.

Why does Peec cap engines at three?

Every Peec self-serve tier includes three of its seven engines; a fourth is a paid add-on ($35–$165/mo), and Claude and GPT-5 Search require Enterprise.
