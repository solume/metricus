Otterly AI Alternatives (2026): 7 AI Visibility Tools Compared

Metricus Research · August 5, 2026 · 4 min read

Teams outgrow Otterly.AI for three reasons: the Lite plan's 15 prompts run out fast, the jump to Standard is a 6.5x price step, and Gemini and Google AI Mode are paid add-ons. The best alternatives in 2026 are Peec AI (more prompts and unlimited seats from $95/mo), Frase (tracking plus a fix workflow from $39/mo), and the Semrush AI Toolkit ($99/mo per domain). To rewrite a specific page and re-test it for citability — which Otterly's GEO audit flags but cannot do — Metricus Forager is the only truly free page-level AI visibility optimizer here. Otterly's own tiers, re-verified at otterly.ai/pricing the week of August 3, 2026: Lite $29/mo (15 prompts), Standard $189/mo (100 prompts), Premium $489/mo (400 prompts), 15% off annually; four base engines, Gemini/AI Mode as add-ons ($9–$149/mo).

1) Peec AI

Peec AI solves Otterly's tightest constraint — prompt volume — with 50/150/350 prompts at $95/$245/$495 and unlimited seats on every tier (verified peec.ai/pricing, August 2026). Three-of-seven engine choice with a fourth as an add-on. Clean dashboards, 7-day trial, no card. It measures rather than fixes, so pair with a page-level optimizer.

2) Frase

Frase answers the exact "Otterly's GEO audit told me what's wrong but gives me nothing to fix it with" complaint: track across five engines, then research, draft, GEO-score and publish the fix — $39/mo yearly, free checker included.

3) Semrush AI Toolkit

The Semrush AI Toolkit offers broader engine coverage inside a full SEO suite for teams already on Semrush: $99/mo per domain, or $199/mo bundled via Semrush One. No free trial on the add-on; a separate free checker exists.

4) Profound

Profound is the enterprise step up from Otterly — first-party crawler data, ten-engine Enterprise coverage, compliance — at a far higher effective price (~$399/mo for real multi-engine coverage). Overkill unless AI visibility is a funded program.

5) AthenaHQ

AthenaHQ adds an action center and attribution on top of tracking, with a free sampler; a mid-market move up from Otterly's monitoring-only model.

6) Writesonic

Writesonic bundles tracking with AI writing; the "free" entry is a 7-day/25-prompt trial, not permanent. Good if you want drafting alongside monitoring.

7) Metricus Forager

Metricus Forager is the free fix for the Otterly gap: it rewrites a page and re-tests it for citability, no card. Use Otterly (or Peec) to find the losing page, then the free AI visibility optimizer to fix it. Documented one-time audit: Metricus Snapshot, $499 one-time, no subscription.

FAQ

How much does Otterly AI cost in 2026?

Lite is $29/mo (15 prompts), Standard $189/mo (100 prompts), Premium $489/mo (400 prompts), with 15% off annual billing. Gemini and Google AI Mode are paid add-ons ($9–$149/mo).

Is Otterly's $29 Lite plan enough?

For a single-brand proof of concept, often yes. Teams outgrow it when 15 prompts run out or when they need Gemini/AI Mode coverage, and the next step up is a 6.5x price jump.

Otterly vs Peec AI — which is better?

Peec offers far more prompts (50–350) and unlimited seats from $95/mo; Otterly is the cheaper entry at $29/mo. Both are monitors — neither rewrites your pages.

What is the cheapest Otterly alternative?

As a paid tracker, nothing undercuts Otterly's $29 Lite. As a free alternative for fixing individual pages, Metricus Forager costs nothing.

Is there a free Otterly alternative?

Yes — Metricus Forager is truly free (no card) for rewriting and re-testing a page, and HubSpot's and Mangools' graders are free for one-time visibility snapshots.

Which Otterly alternative actually fixes pages?

Metricus Forager (free, page-level). Frase and Writesonic include paid content workflows; the pure trackers report only.

Which engines does Otterly cover?

Four base engines — ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Copilot — with Gemini and Google AI Mode available as paid add-ons.
