How to Get Recommended by ChatGPT (2026)

Metricus Research · August 5, 2026 · 4 min read

To get recommended by ChatGPT, make your page the easiest source to quote for the exact question a buyer asks: open with a one-sentence direct answer, add specific statistics and cited sources, structure the page with clear headings and an FAQ, and keep it fresh. This is not a guess — the peer-reviewed Princeton/Georgia Tech/IIT Delhi study (Aggarwal et al., KDD 2024) found that adding statistics lifted a source's visibility in generative answers by up to 41%, adding quotations by ~28%, and citing sources by up to 115% for lower-ranked pages, while keyword stuffing performed worse than making no change at all. And ranking #1 is not enough: Surfer's November 28, 2025 study (Joshua Hardwick, 173,902 URLs) found 67.82% of AI Overview citations don't rank in Google's top 10, meaning citation-shaped content — not just rank — is what wins the mention.

The 7 steps

1. Lead with a one-sentence direct answer

Put the answer to the target question in the first sentence, in plain language, before any preamble — answer engines quote the passage that most cleanly resolves the query. If a reader can't tell what the page's answer is from sentence one, neither can a model assembling a response.

2. Add specific statistics with sources

The single highest-return change in the only peer-reviewed GEO study: statistics addition, +41% visibility (Aggarwal et al., KDD 2024). Replace vague claims ("many teams," "significant growth") with attributed numbers, and date them.

3. Add quotations and cite sources

Quotation addition (~28%) and citing external sources (up to +115% for lower-ranked pages) were the next strongest levers in the same study. A page that shows its evidence reads as quotable evidence itself.

4. Structure for extraction

Use descriptive H2/H3s, short paragraphs, a comparison table where relevant, and an FAQ — the passage shapes engines reach for. One idea per paragraph; the table and FAQ give a model self-contained blocks it can lift.

5. Cover the fan-out, not just the head term

Surfer found pages ranking for fan-out queries are 161% more likely to be cited; answer the sub-questions a buyer would ask next (pricing, comparisons, "is it worth it," "for whom"), on the same page or tightly linked ones.

6. Keep it fresh and consistent

Freshness correlates with citations; keep facts, prices and dates current and consistent across the page, and show a visible "last verified" date. Stale numbers get a page quietly dropped from answers.

FAQ

Can I pay ChatGPT to recommend my business?

No. There is no paid placement in ChatGPT's organic answers; recommendations come from what the model retrieves and what it learned. The lever you control is making your pages the most quotable source for your buyers' questions.

How long does it take to get recommended?

Edited, already-indexed pages can begin appearing in AI answers within days to weeks; new pages take longer. Re-test on a 14–30 day window rather than daily.

Does schema markup help?

It supports extraction — FAQ and HowTo markup mirror the passage shapes engines quote — but it cannot rescue a page whose visible text lacks a direct answer, statistics and structure. Do the content first; add the markup that matches it.

What kind of content does ChatGPT cite most?

Content with a direct answer up top, attributed statistics, comparison tables, FAQs and fresh dates — the exact features measured in the KDD 2024 study and observed across citation analyses.

Is ranking #1 on Google enough?

No. Surfer's 2025 analysis of 173,902 URLs found 67.82% of AI Overview citations came from pages outside Google's top 10. Rank helps; citation-shaped content decides.

Statistics or keywords — which matters more?
