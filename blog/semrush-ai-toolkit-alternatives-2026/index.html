Semrush AI Toolkit Alternatives (2026): 7 Tools, Ranked and Priced

Metricus Research · August 5, 2026 · 3 min read

The Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit is a $99/mo-per-domain add-on — not a free feature of core Semrush and not, on its own, a full AEO program. If you do not already live in Semrush, the best alternatives in 2026 are Peec AI (dedicated tracking, unlimited seats, $95/mo), Frase (track plus fix, $39/mo), and Otterly.AI ($29/mo to start). To fix a specific page for citability without any subscription, Metricus Forager is the only truly free page-level AI visibility optimizer here. Verified the week of August 3, 2026: the toolkit is $99/mo per domain (25 prompts), +$60/mo per 50 extra prompts, +$99/mo per extra domain, no free trial; it can be bought standalone or bundled into Semrush One (Starter $199/mo).

Standalone or add-on? The pricing, resolved

Sources conflict on whether the toolkit requires a Semrush base plan. Vendor and multiple independent trackers (verified August 2026) confirm it is available standalone at $99/mo per domain, and can also be bundled via Semrush One. Earlier "requires a base plan" claims are outdated. There is also a separate free one-time AI-visibility checker at semrush.com/free-tools that needs no subscription at all.

1) Peec AI

Peec AI is the strongest dedicated alternative when AI visibility is your primary metric, not one KPI in a suite: $95/$245/$495 for 50/150/350 prompts, unlimited seats, 7-day trial. Where the Semrush toolkit charges $99 per domain and $60 per 50 extra prompts, Peec's per-project economics scale more cleanly for single-brand programs.

2) Frase

Frase replaces "AI data in a dashboard" with "AI data that drives a content fix" — track five engines, then draft and publish the fix, from $39/mo yearly with a free checker.

3) Otterly.AI

Otterly.AI is the cheapest starting point at $29/mo (15 prompts), rising to $189/$489. Four engines included; Gemini/AI Mode add-ons. A validation entry before committing budget.

4) Profound

Profound is the enterprise alternative with first-party crawler data and compliance the Semrush add-on doesn't match — but at ~$399/mo for real multi-engine coverage.

5) AthenaHQ

AthenaHQ pairs tracking with attribution and an action center, plus a free sampler; a dedicated-tool step up from a suite add-on.

6) Writesonic

Writesonic bundles tracking with AI writing; the "free" entry is a 7-day/25-prompt trial.

7) Metricus Forager

Metricus Forager is the free page-fixer none of the above (or Semrush) includes: rewrite a page and re-test it for citability with the free AI visibility optimizer, no card. Documented one-time audit: Metricus Snapshot, $499 one-time, no subscription.

FAQ

Is the Semrush AI Toolkit available standalone?

Yes. It can be purchased standalone at $99/mo per domain, or bundled into Semrush One (Starter $199/mo). Earlier claims that it requires a core Semrush plan are outdated.

How much does the Semrush AI Toolkit cost per domain?

$99/mo per domain for 25 tracked prompts, plus $60/mo per additional 50 prompts and $99/mo per additional domain. There is no free trial for the add-on.

Is there a free Semrush AI visibility checker?

Yes — a separate free one-time checker at semrush.com/free-tools, independent of the paid toolkit.

What is the best dedicated alternative to the Semrush AI Toolkit?

Peec AI, if AI visibility is your primary metric: 50–350 prompts, unlimited seats, from $95/mo.

What is the cheapest alternative?

Otterly.AI at $29/mo as a paid tracker; Metricus Forager free for fixing individual pages.

Does any alternative rewrite pages instead of just tracking?

Metricus Forager does, for free. Frase adds a paid content-fix workflow; the pure trackers report only.
