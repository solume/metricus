Run it on a client's page. Show them the answer.

The Metricus optimizer is free for every page. Put in the client's URL and you have the answer an AI assistant gives about that page, the passages that caused it, a rewrite for each one, and the answer after the rewrites, side by side.

Before the pitch

Run it on the prospect's main product, service or pricing page. Bring the answer to the first call. The prospect sees their own page the way an assistant reads it.

In the meeting

Walk through the marked passages and the rewrites. Change one on screen and watch the answer move.

After

Take the changes as a file: the page before and after as a diff, a CSV of the changes, the findings as markdown, or print. Paste the rewrites into the client's site yourself or hand them to their team, then run it again.

What it costs

Nothing, for one client page or a hundred. No signup, no card, no fee per client, no reseller tier, because nothing is sold. What you charge for the work around it is your call.

A presentation template for the client meeting and a short guide to using the optimizer with clients are on the agency resources pages.

Questions

Can I use it on client pages?

Yes. Put in any public page. It runs the same way for every page, and there is no limit on how many pages you run.

What do I show the client?

The answer an AI assistant gives with their page as a source, the passages of the page that caused it, the rewrites, and the answer after the rewrites, side by side. Screenshot it or run it on screen.

Do I need access to the client's site?

No. The tool opens the public page the way any reader does. Nothing on the site changes. You or the client decide what to publish.

Can I present the result under my own brand?

Yes. The result is data about the client's page. Put it in your own deck, in your own words. Your recommendation is the deliverable; the run is the evidence behind it.

What do I hand to someone who was not in the meeting?

The findings file: the prompt, the answer today, the passages behind it, and every change you kept. Or the page before and after as a diff, or the changes as a CSV.
