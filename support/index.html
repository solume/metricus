Common questions

How much does the optimizer cost?

Nothing. No signup, no card, no subscription.

How do I run it?

Put the address of a page into the tool and press Start optimizing. The page fills in from the top.

What does it show me?

The answer an AI assistant gives with your page among its sources, the passages of your page that caused that answer, and a rewrite for each one. Change anything and the assistant is asked again.

What if AI does not mention my brand at all?

That is the most common finding. The tool places your page among the results so the assistant can read it at all, and the line above the answer says so. The marks then show which passages hold the answer back.

Something did not work. Who do I contact?

Email metricusapp@gmail.com with the address you put in and what you saw.
