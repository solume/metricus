Common questions

How much does Metricus cost?

Nothing. No signup, no card, no subscription.

How do I use it?

Type the question a customer would ask AI, in the customer's words. Then pick your website from the businesses the answer names, or add its address.

What does it show me?

The answer AI gives, with every business it names and a link to each website. You edit the answer into what you want AI to say about you, then get new text for your website and see the answer AI gives with it next to today's. The AI Visibility Score shows how often AI mentions and recommends you, today and with the new text.

What if AI does not mention my brand at all?

Add your website's address to the list of businesses in the answer. Then edit the answer into what you want AI to say about you, and you get new text for your website.

Something did not work. Who do I contact?

Email metricusapp@gmail.com with the question you typed and what you saw.
